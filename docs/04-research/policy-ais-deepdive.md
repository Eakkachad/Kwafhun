## 1. กสศ. (EEF) programs & scale

- Per-student rate has risen in steps: 1,600 baht/student/year (2561/2018) → 3,000 baht/student/year (2563/2020) → 3,480 baht/student/year (2567/2024) → **4,200 baht/student/year (2,100/semester) for 2569/2026**. [VERIFIED] — [bangkokbiznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/news-update/1137901), [eef.or.th CCT page](https://www.eef.or.th/fund/support-poor-students/) (2025/2026)

[VERIFIED] — [eef.or.th news](https://www.eef.or.th/news-270526/) (2026), [thestandard.co](https://thestandard.co/equity-fund-disbursement-students/) (~800,000 in first disbursement round mentioned separately — note the 800k figure is for a specific disbursement window, not total annual beneficiaries; don't conflate)

**iSEE database**
- iSEE (Information System for Equitable Education) links students' 13-digit national ID with data from 6 ministries (Education, Social Development & Human Security, Public Health, Interior, Finance, Prime Minister's Office/NDB) — parental income, household status, home-condition photos, health data, risk behaviors, learning needs, attendance, academic results, plus GIS. [VERIFIED] — [eef.or.th/isee-eef](https://www.eef.or.th/isee-eef/) (article dated 2019, describes initial rollout)
- Coverage: originally screened ~500,000 extremely poor students via 3-tier verification (parent/teacher/school committee); system's full target scope is "more than 4 million children and youth lacking resources," across all ~30,000 educational institutions nationwide. [VERIFIED, but note the 4M figure is a stated target/design scope, not confirmed current coverage — flag as design capacity, not necessarily realized count] — same source, 2019.
- Newer iSEE 2.0 reportedly scans "1.7 million" poor students with compounded hardship factors per ThaiPublica (2020). [VERIFIED as reported] — [thaipublica.org](https://thaipublica.org/2020/09/eef-isee-version2-02/) (2020)

## 3. TCAS / ทปอ. / mytcas — Portfolio round & equity critique

- Portfolio round is criticized for favoring resourced students: students at well-known urban schools have advantages in funding, networks, and opportunity vs. small-town/rural school students; a "port-building business" (ธุรกิจปั้นพอร์ต) — camps, workshops, specialized coaching — has grown yearly. [VERIFIED, reported] — [trueid.net](https://news.trueid.net/detail/pYRMLwn56JQK) (2026), [today.line.me citing สภาพัฒน์/NESDC concern](https://today.line.me/th/v3/article/BEqNpBj) (2026)
- สภาพัฒน์ (NESDC) explicitly flagged concern that TCAS Portfolio round risks worsening inequality — "คัดคนมีเงินทิ้งคนเก่ง" (selects those with money, leaves behind the talented). [VERIFIED] — [today.line.me](https://today.line.me/th/v3/article/BEqNpBj) (2026)
- รมว.อว. (Minister of Higher Education, Science, Research and Innovation) has stated the Portfolio round risks inequality and is considering asking universities to reduce Portfolio-round admission quotas starting 2570 (2027). [VERIFIED, reported] — [eduzones.com](https://www.eduzones.com/2026/01/05/portfolio-70/) (2026)
- ทปอ. (Council of University Presidents of Thailand) response: **TCASFolio** — a free, centralized digital portfolio standard/platform meant to reduce unnecessary portfolio-padding and reduce the commercial "port-building" incentive. Also a discussion of freezing/reducing TCAS application fees and a **25% discount on Portfolio-round application fees for students in the กสศ. system**. [VERIFIED, reported] — [ptp.or.th](https://www.ptp.or.th/archives/48679) (2026), [dekshowport.com on TCASFolio](https://dekshowport.com/tcasfolio-%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%B7%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%B0%E0%B9%84%E0%B8%A3-%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%B4%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%AB%E0%B9%8C%E0%B9%81%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%A7%E0%B9%82/) (2026)
- **This directly supports our "screening in-class for equity" pitch angle**: there is now an official (ทปอ.-level) acknowledgment that Portfolio-round mechanics disadvantage under-resourced students, and a concrete precedent (25% fee discount for กสศ.-registered students) of policy makers already carving out equity accommodations in the TCAS pipeline. [VERIFIED — good citable talking point]

## 6. PDPA & children's data — CONFIRMED, strong for Two-Tier model

- **พ.ร.บ. คุ้มครองข้อมูลส่วนบุคคล พ.ศ. 2562 (PDPA), มาตรา 20 (Section 20)**: governs consent for ผู้เยาว์ (minors). [VERIFIED] — [pdpathailand.com/pdpa/content/article20.php](https://pdpathailand.com/pdpa/content/article20.php)
- Effective consent structure:
  - Minor aged **10 or under**: consent must be obtained from the parent/guardian (ผู้ใช้อำนาจปกครอง) alone.
  - Minor aged **over 10 (up to legal majority)**: consent must be obtained from **both** the minor AND the parent/guardian jointly — except for acts a minor may consent to alone under มาตรา 22/23/24 of the Civil and Commercial Code. [VERIFIED] — same source.
- **Note on the brief's "under-15" framing**: PDPA's actual break-point in มาตรา 20 is age **10**, not 15 — the law does not use 15 as a threshold. The brief's phrasing ("under-15 consent... parental consent requirement") is directionally correct (minors broadly need parental involvement) but the specific age-10 threshold should replace any "under-15" framing in deck copy to avoid a legal-accuracy overclaim. [VERIFIED CORRECTION — use age 10, not 15, as the PDPA breakpoint]
- Practical implication for our target age band (ป.5–ม.3 ≈ ages ~10–15): **essentially the entire user base requires dual consent (student + parent/guardian)** under PDPA, since almost all users are over age 10. This actually strengthens (not weakens) the case for a Two-Tier data model with mandatory parental consent flows built in from day one. [VERIFIED — good, more precise citation for deck]
