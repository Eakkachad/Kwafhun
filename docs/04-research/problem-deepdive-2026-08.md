## 3. CONTRADICTIONS / THINGS TO RECONCILE

1. **1.8M vs 1.39M/1.3M extremely poor students**: Our deck's "1.8M extremely poor students" figure is notably higher than the most current กสศ. ทุนเสมอภาค screening numbers found this session (1.39M for AY2568/2025, 1.3M planned for AY2569/2026). This could be a legitimate difference (e.g., 1.8M = total poor+extremely poor children nationally, vs. 1.3-1.4M = specifically screened/funded through the equity scholarship this particular year), but it needs reconciliation against the primary กสศ. source the original figure came from, especially since กสศ.'s own numbers appear to be trending DOWN in recent years — using an older, higher number without noting the trend could be seen as overclaiming in a strict-discipline deck.
2. **TDRI attribution error**: If the current deck attributes the 3.3 trillion THB figure to TDRI, this should be corrected to **World Bank + EEF (ASAT 2022 study)** — a different (and arguably more authoritative, since it's World Bank co-authored) source. TDRI has its own separate, smaller, genuine figure (300k+ unemployed grads/year, 10B+ THB opportunity cost) that could be used instead if a TDRI citation specifically is wanted.
3. **NESDC 300-500k figure**: could not directly locate a NESDC document stating "300,000-500,000" workers shortage. Found instead two different NESDC-adjacent figures: (a) NXPO's 1.08M/2025-2029 sector-specific figure, and (b) NESDC's own 44.71M demand vs 40.7M supply by 2037 gap. **Recommend the team locate the original NESDC document for the 300-500k figure to confirm it's real and not a rounding/misremembering of one of these other two**, or switch to the NXPO figure which is well-sourced and more precise.
4. **PISA "47-50% narrow to 10 careers"**: as flagged above, this may actually be the OECD AVERAGE rather than a Thailand-specific figure. Not necessarily wrong to cite as context, but should be labeled "OECD average" rather than implied as Thailand's own number, unless Thailand's specific PISA Vol III figure can be confirmed (blocked by 403 this session).
